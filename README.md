# CareCore SaaS
> UK Care Home Management Platform — Design & Architecture

A fully compliant, cloud-native SaaS platform purpose-built for UK care homes.
Replacing paper-based workflows, unifying operations, and enabling proactive care
through data and AI.

## Quick Reference

**Target Market**: UK registered care homes (residential, nursing, EMI/dementia)

**Regulatory Framework**: CQC, GDPR/DPA 2018, Mental Capacity Act 2005, Care Act 2014

**Data Residency**: AWS eu-west-2 (London) — UK only

**Phase 1 MVP**: Resident management, Digital MAR, Incident reporting, Basic billing

---

## Core Modules

1. Resident Intake & Management
2. Staff & Rota Management
3. Care Delivery & Safety (MAR, tasks, IoT)
4. Inventory & Supplies Management
5. Finance & Billing
6. Compliance & Reporting (CQC-aligned)
